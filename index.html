It was a beautiful day and I feel a bit salty and lit having been honest and forethright through the day.  Could I have done better?  The answer is always "Yes".  But I believe my effort is pleasing to God.

I am grateful for getting to hear birds chirping when I woke up.  (I used to hate hearing them because they made it harder to go back to sleep.)

I am grateful for those that don't treat A.A. meetings as place to trauma dump. (I'm not saying sharing about shouldn't be done, but I whole-heartedly believe it should be done outside of a meeting or with qualified personnel.)

I am grateful for the people that can help others in ways I can't or won't. (When it actually helps more than it harms.)

I am grateful for being okay with comforting the uncomfortable and making the comfortable uncomfortable.  (You're welcome.  :-D)

I am grateful for being able to admit that I don't have all the answers. (But I know one that does.  His name is God...)

I am grateful for being able to see that when I start thinking a lot, it's because fear exists somewhere, and it's my responsibility to ask God to remove it. (I tried way too hard to hide from my feelings for too long to think any other solution will remedy this malady.)

I am grateful that I don't have to drink today, nor do I have to suffer through the feelings that come with not drinking today. (See the last line...)
